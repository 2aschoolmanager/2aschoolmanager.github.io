      Before you buy

      Can I try it first?

      Yes. Download it or open the web app, set up your school and use it free for 30 days, with every feature and up to 3 users. When you are ready, enter your licence key and carry on with the same records.

      Who counts as a user?

      Each member of staff who signs in, such as the administrator, headteacher, bursar, teachers and the librarian. Parents use the parent portal with their own code and are not counted.

      How do I get a licence key?

      Message us your school's name, the number of users and the period you want. We confirm the price, you pay by Mobile Money, and once the payment is confirmed your key is sent to your email. In the app, open Admin, Licence and paste it in. A key belongs to one school and lasts for the period you paid for.

      We are an Arabic or Islamic school. Does it work for us?

      Yes. Choose Arabic only, or English and Arabic, when you set up the school. Names, subjects, grade bands, report cards and positions work in Arabic, and screens read right to left. There is a Qur'an and Hifz tracker too.

      Can we use the GES grading and BECE grades?

      Yes. The app comes with ready-made grade scales (GES basic, BECE 1 to 9, WASSCE A1 to F9 and Arabic bands), and you can change them or make your own for each level. You choose the marking scheme too: out of 100, or class score plus exam score with your own split.

      How do parents pay fees with MoMo?

      The school connects its own Paystack account in the app. Parents then tap Pay with MoMo in the parent portal, approve the payment on their phone, and the receipt appears by itself. The money goes into the school's account, not ours.

      Is our school's data kept separate from other schools?

      Yes. Each school's records are stored separately and only the people you invite can open them. Parents see only their own children.

      Is there a manual?

      Yes. Download the user manual from the download section, as a PDF or in Word. It covers every screen with screenshots, plus a two-page handout for parents.

      Can we bring our students in from Excel?

      Yes. Download the template from the Import screen, fill it in (or paste from your old sheet) and import the whole school at once.

      What happens if the internet goes off?

      The app keeps a saved copy, so you can still open and read your records. Changes can be made again when the internet is back.

      Windows says it protected my PC. Is the app safe?

      Windows shows this notice for any new program that has not built up a download history yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your school's data is kept in the cloud under your own login.

      Why does my phone warn me about the Android app?

      Chrome and Google Play Protect show these warnings for any app installed from a website instead of the Play Store. Tap Download anyway, then More details and Install anyway. On an iPhone, open the web app and add it to your home screen.

      Can we add more users later?

      Yes. Ask us to add users and you pay only the difference for the time left on your licence.
